# Pastel — how this site is built

This is the **built** form of the template *Pastel*: tall cards in pink,
butter and lilac with a price bar across the foot, and headings that change
weight mid-line. It was built to the owner's picture (`design/template.jpg`),
and a project that picks the template starts from it.

There is no direction to choose here. What is left to decide is how one
business sits in it:

- **no photograph opens the page**: the first screen is the heading, the
  proof beside it and the top of three tinted cards. The colour is in the
  cards and nowhere else — the page is white and the type is one dark colour;
- a **heading changes weight inside the line** (`<Weights>`): a light run, a
  bold run. There is no second face and no coloured type;
- the hero's **pill sits ON the heading's last line**, beside its words;
- the things on tinted cards are **photographs on a plain pale backdrop**,
  filling the card's picture — nothing is cut out, and a busy room on a tint
  is a rectangle on a colour;
- the proof beside the heading is **counted, not claimed**: how many things
  are in the shop, read from the records. This system has nobody's reviews;
- the four tints and the ink may be re-tinted to the business — the tints
  stay pale, the page stays white.

What the template has that this system cannot make: other people's review
counts and partner logos. Where the logos were, the departments stand as
wordmarks; where the review count was, the shop's own count.

## COMMITMENTS

- The first screen has no photograph: a heading on three lines whose words change between a light and a bold weight, with a dark pill sitting on its last line beside the words.
- At the heading's right is a short rule, three overlapping round pictures, a count in bold and two lines of small text.
- Under the heading are three tall rounded cards, each filled with one pale tint — pink, butter, lilac — with a photograph of the thing on it, a small white pill at its top left, its name large, and a bar of glass across its foot carrying the price and a round dark button.
- The header is one row between two hairlines that stop at the page's measure.
- The departments stand as grey letterspaced wordmarks in one row between two hairlines.
- A peach rounded panel holds three reassurances side by side, each a round mark, a bold label and a line.
- Everything else is small cards four to a row: a pale rounded tile with a heart, the name and price on one line, and an outlined pill under them.
